How they're made

The two 3D pieces use three.js r186 with the WebGPU renderer and TSL node materials, and fall back to WebGL 2 where WebGPU isn't available. The almanac is plain SVG projected by hand; the towers are Canvas 2D. Every palette was checked for colour-blind separation, and each piece has a data table.

Read with care

The land-cover layout and the metro station positions are schematic. Shares, dates, lengths and counts are as published. City boundaries changed between censuses, most of all in 2007.
