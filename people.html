Lakhs of People

Every cube is one lakh (1,00,000) people. Bengaluru went from 4 lakh in 1941 to 84 lakh in 2011, about twenty-one times over. Cubes keep the colour of the decade that added them, so each tower shows its own history.

Added in the decade · hover to trace a layer

The 2001–2011 layer includes people counted inside the city after the 2007 merger that formed the BBMP, which absorbed 7 city councils, 1 town council and 110 villages.

Source: Census of India, via the population table on Wikipedia. City boundaries changed between censuses.
